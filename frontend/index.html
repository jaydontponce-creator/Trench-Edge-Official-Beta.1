How Trench Edge Works

Purpose

Trench Edge estimates college football scoring, compares the projection with the betting market, and decides whether the difference is strong enough to qualify as a wager.

How picks are made

The model combines efficiency, line play, pace, explosiveness, first-down creation, turnovers, special teams, home field, weather, availability, continuity, opponent context, data quality, and market price.

Risk

Risk measures how vulnerable a projection is to realistic game events such as pressure, sacks, turnovers, injuries, special-teams swings, weather, or an unusual game script.
