How Trench Edge Works

What is Trench Edge?

Trench Edge is a college-football prediction and market-analysis model. It estimates scoring, compares its projection with sportsbook prices, and decides whether the difference is strong and reliable enough to qualify as a wager.

How are picks made?

TE combines team efficiency, rushing and passing performance, line play, pace, explosiveness, first-down creation, turnovers, special teams, home field, weather, injuries, roster continuity, opponent context, data quality, and market price. A prediction can look strong and still be a pass if the betting price does not offer enough value.

What does risk mean?

Risk is the chance that realistic game events push the outcome away from the central projection. Pressure, sacks, turnovers, stalled drives, short fields, special-teams mistakes, injuries, weather, or unusual game scripts can all change a game. Risk does not mean the model expects to be wrong. It measures how vulnerable a prediction is to plausible sources of variance.
